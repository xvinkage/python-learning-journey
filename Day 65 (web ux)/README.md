## 💡 What I Learned

### 🎨 Color Theory
Color theory is the art and science of choosing colors that work well together and communicate the right message.

- Consider the mood and dominant colors of a design.
- Colors can convey meaning and emotion:
  - Blue: Trust and reliability
  - Red: Energy and excitement
  - Purple: Wealth and luxury
- **Analogous colors:** Colors next to each other on the color wheel. They create harmony and work well for backgrounds and logos.
- **Complementary colors:** Colors opposite each other on the color wheel. They create contrast and make elements stand out. They work well for logos and icons but should be used carefully with text.

### 🔤 Typography
Typography is the art of arranging text to make it readable and communicate a message.

- Limit a design to two font families when possible.
- Fonts convey moods and emotions.
- Different typeface categories:
  - **Serif:** Traditional, respectable, authoritative. Serif fonts have small strokes or “feet” at the ends of letters.
  - **Sans-serif:** Simple, sensible, straightforward. Commonly used by startups and modern businesses.
  - **Script:** Creative and elegant.
  - **Display:** Loud, amusing, and friendly.
  - **Modern:** Chic, smart, and stylish.

### 🖥️ User Interface (UI) Design
UI design focuses on the visual elements users interact with.

- **Visual hierarchy:** Use color, size, and placement to emphasize important elements.
- **Layout:** Aim for approximately 40–60 characters per line for comfortable readability.
- **Alignment:** Keep elements aligned to create an organized appearance.
- **White space:** Empty space helps reduce clutter and can create a luxurious, minimalist appearance.
- **Audience:** Consider the intended audience when selecting colors, layouts, and visual elements.

### 🧭 User Experience (UX) Design
UX design focuses on how users interact with a product and how easy and enjoyable that experience is.

- **Invisible and unobtrusive:** Good UX should feel natural rather than distracting.
- **Simplicity:** Make tasks easy to understand and complete.
- **Consistency:** Keep design and functionality consistent throughout the application.
- **F-pattern layout:** Users often scan content horizontally near the top and then vertically down the left side. Important information should be positioned accordingly.
- **Z-pattern layout:** Users tend to scan from top-left to top-right, diagonally down, and then across the bottom. This can be useful for landing pages.

## 🎯 Key Takeaway
Good web design is more than making a website look attractive. It combines color, typography, layout, and usability to create an intuitive experience for the user.
